New Opportunities Waiver (NOW)

For people with developmental disabilities

A Medicaid waiver that provides home and community-based supports to people with developmental disabilities who need the level of care of an intermediate care facility (ICF/ID).

NOW offers an alternative to institutional care and promotes independence and community inclusion. It is built on the principles of self-determination and supplements the family and community supports already in place.

Services include

  • Residential supports
  • Respite
  • Community integration and development
  • Employment-related supports
  • Habilitation
  • Environmental modifications and specialized equipment
  • Professional services
  • And more

Children's Choice Waiver

For children from birth through age 18

A Medicaid Home and Community-Based Services program that offers supplemental support to children with developmental disabilities who live at home with their families, or who will leave an institution to return home. Each child receives a set annual amount for support services, including support coordination, in a package designed for maximum flexibility.

Services include

  • Center-based respite
  • Family support
  • Environmental accessibility adaptations
  • Support coordination
  • Specialized medical equipment and supplies
  • Aquatic therapy
  • Art therapy
  • Music therapy
  • Sensory integration
  • Hippotherapy (therapeutic horseback riding)
  • Housing stabilization services
  • Housing stabilization transition and family training
How the waiver works

This optional waiver is offered to as many children as funding allows. Families of children on the Developmental Disabilities Request for Services Registry (DDRFSR) can accept a Children's Choice Waiver offer or remain on the registry. The waiver is operated by the Office for Citizens with Developmental Disabilities (OCDD) under the authorization of the Bureau of Health Services Financing (BHSF), both within the Louisiana Department of Health.

Support services are specified in the Plan of Care (POC), developed by a person-centered planning team: support coordinators, service providers, family or guardians, and those who know the child best. The POC covers all services and activities, including non-waiver services.

These services supplement all other medically necessary Medicaid services. Recipients receive only the support services in the POC approved by the local governing entity (LGE).

Community Choices Waiver

For seniors and adults with adult-onset disabilities

A Medicaid Home and Community-Based Services waiver that provides alternative services so people can live in the community instead of a nursing facility or institution.

How the waiver works

Services are specified in a Plan of Care written by the support coordinator with input from the planning team, then sent to OAAS or its designee for approval. The planning team includes the recipient, the support coordinator and, as the recipient prefers, family members, natural supports, professionals and others the recipient chooses.

The Plan of Care covers all waiver and non-waiver services. Recipients receive the waiver services in the plan approved by the support coordination designee or OAAS regional office. The support coordinator notifies the provider of approved services, and the data management agency issues prior authorization.

Long Term Personal Care Services (LT-PCS)

For adults at risk of nursing facility placement

An optional Medicaid State Plan service for recipients who need help with activities of daily living and are either in a nursing home or at imminent risk of nursing facility placement.

LT-PCS must be prior authorized, follow an approved Plan of Care, and be coordinated with other Medicaid services, community services and informal supports. Each person requesting LT-PCS completes a functional eligibility screening.

EPSDT Personal Care Services

For eligible recipients up to age 21

The Early and Periodic Screening, Diagnosis and Treatment (EPSDT) program provides personal care services to eligible recipients up to age 21 who meet the medically necessary criteria. Services are provided by a Medicaid-enrolled agency with a valid Personal Care Attendant License.

EPSDT PCS does not include medical tasks such as medication administration, tracheostomy care, feeding tubes or catheters. Those are requested through the Home Health Program or, for waiver recipients, through the waiver program.

Behavioral Health Personal Care Services (BHPCS)

For people receiving behavioral health care

BHPCS helps participants who cannot qualify for traditional Home and Community Based Services. A Direct Service Worker comes to you and helps with dressing, cooking, light housekeeping, laundry, medication reminders, walking and transferring.

If you receive behavioral health care services, call us and our Behavioral Health Specialist will do an on-site assessment.

Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT)

Rides to scheduled health care appointments

We pick participants up and take them to their health care provider for scheduled appointments. We currently accept Louisiana Healthcare Connections and AmeriHealth.

To book, call the 800 number on the back of your insurance card and request Autumn Lake Transportation as your provider.
