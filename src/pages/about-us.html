Care that helps people live well at home.

Autumn Lake LLC is a leading provider of home health care for families across the Baton Rouge area.

Our mission

To provide quality services through Autumn Lake's tradition of care, which strives to preserve dignity, nurture the spirit, celebrate individuality, enable freedom of choice, encourage independence, and involve family and friends.

Our vision

Autumn Lake embraces growth as we seek to provide individualized care and services to those we serve.

Why Autumn Lake exists.

Autumn Lake was created to build stronger communities through quality personal care services. We do this through assessments, community support, individualized plans of care, individual interventions, specialized Client Specific Training, and referrals to other agencies.

We work to improve quality of life for our elderly and disabled population by teaching skills that help clients function well at home and in the community. Our experienced team develops each service plan alongside the client and their family, with the goal of reducing and preventing hospitalization.

A healthy, growing community creates jobs, safety, and a bright future for everyone involved. We want to help build that community by breaking barriers and empowering people.

Six commitments behind every visit.

  • Preserve dignity

    Care delivered with respect for every person's privacy and pride.

  • Nurture the spirit

    Attention to emotional well-being, not only physical needs.

  • Celebrate individuality

    Plans built around each client's routines, history and preferences.

  • Enable freedom of choice

    Clients and families decide what care looks like.

  • Encourage independence

    Teaching skills that help clients do more for themselves.

  • Involve family and friends

    The people who know the client best are part of the plan.
