Stronger communities start with care at home.

Autumn Lake was created to build stronger communities through quality personal care services. We work to improve quality of life for our elderly and disabled neighbors by teaching skills that help clients live well at home and in the community.

Our experienced team builds a service plan alongside each client and their family, so everyone understands it, with the goal of reducing and preventing hospitalization.
